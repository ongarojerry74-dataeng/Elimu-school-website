Our Story

A Foundation Built on Purpose

Elimu School was founded in 2001 by a group of passionate educators determined to provide an alternative to rote learning — one that develops the whole child: intellectually, emotionally, and socially. What began as a modest primary school of 80 pupils has grown into a thriving institution of over 1,200 students across primary and secondary levels.

Our philosophy is simple: every child possesses untapped potential, and it is our privilege to unlock it. We combine the rigour of a high-performing examination school with the warmth of a community that genuinely knows each learner by name.

Est. 2001

Our Journey

Milestones Through the Years

From a single classroom to a landmark institution — every step forward has been shaped by our community.

  1. 2001

    School Founded

    Elimu School opens its doors with 800 pupils and 60 dedicated teachers in Nairobi's Westlands.

  2. 2008

    First Graduating Class

    Our inaugural Form 4 class achieves a 94% mean grade — the start of a proud examination tradition.

  3. 2015

    New Science Block Opens

    A state-of-the-art science and technology wing with four fully equipped laboratories is inaugurated.

  4. 2020

    Digital Learning Launch

    Elimu becomes one of the first schools in the region to deploy a full blended-learning platform and 1:1 device programme.

  5. 2025

    Top Performing School Award

    Named Kenya's Top Performing Mixed-Day School by the Ministry of Education — a testament to our community's dedication.

Admissions FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying to Elimu School.

Applications for the following academic year open every September and close on 30 November. We recommend applying early as places are competitive, especially for Grade 1 and Grade 7 entry points.

All applicants sit a written assessment in English and Mathematics. Senior secondary applicants also provide their most recent school report and one teacher's reference. Assessments are held in January and are free of charge.

Yes. Elimu School awards merit and need-based bursaries covering 25–100 % of tuition fees. Applications are considered alongside the academic application. We believe financial constraints should never prevent a talented child from joining us.

Elimu operates as a day school. We do, however, run a supervised study programme from 4 pm to 7 pm Monday–Friday for students who need a structured environment for homework and revision after class.

We host open-day tours on the last Saturday of each month during term time. Families can register via email or telephone. Private visits for families with specific access needs can be arranged by contacting the admissions office directly.
