Academic Programmes

A Curriculum for Every Stage

Structured academic pathways supporting learners from primary through senior secondary with age-appropriate rigour and care.

Primary School (Grade 1–6)

Our primary programme lays a strong literacy and numeracy foundation while nurturing natural curiosity through project-based learning. Small class sizes ensure every child gets individual attention.

Pastoral care, mother-tongue language support, and an active sports and arts programme complete a joyful, holistic early education.

Subjects Offered

  • English
  • Kiswahili
  • Mathematics
  • Science & Technology
  • Social Studies
  • Creative Arts
  • Physical Education
  • Religious Education

Junior Secondary (Grade 7–9)

Aligned with the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), this level deepens subject knowledge while building critical thinking, digital literacy, and collaborative skills.

Students explore career pathways: Arts & Sports Science, Social Sciences, STEM, and Technical & Applied Studies.

Subjects Offered

  • English
  • Kiswahili
  • Mathematics
  • Integrated Science
  • Social Studies
  • Agriculture
  • Business Studies
  • ICT
  • Creative Arts
  • French
  • German
  • Music
  • Aviation Studies

Senior Secondary (Grade 10–12)

Students select a specialisation pathway and sit national examinations that open doors to Kenya's top universities and global institutions. Our pass rate consistently outperforms the national average.

Dedicated university guidance, interview coaching, and scholarship support are available to all Form 4 students.
